# Interview preparation

## What you need
Company · role · interview type (phone / technical / case / final round) · date.
Ask for whatever is missing. Read the CV and the posting too if they exist.

## 1. Research the company: search, never write from memory
- What they do, where the revenue comes from, who the customers are
- News from the last six months: funding, a product launch, layoffs, a change of
  direction
- Competitors, and where they differ
- Where possible, the role and background of the person conducting the interview

Put a source on every finding. Do not invent what you could not find.

## 2. Question bank
**Behavioural** (8-10), matched to the competencies the role calls for: a
difficult situation, conflict, failure, leadership, ambiguity, competing
priorities.

**Technical or domain** (depending on the role): at least one question for each
required qualification in the posting.

**Company-specific**: "why us", "what would you do in the first 90 days in this
role", and a current topic that came out of your research.

## 3. Build the stories with STAR
Turn 4-6 strong experiences from the CV into stories:
**Situation** (two sentences) → **Task** → **Action** (the substance, in the
first person) → **Result** (with a number where there is one)

No story should run past 90 seconds. One story can answer more than one
question; map which fits where.

**Prepare for the weak spots:** a gap in the CV, a short stint, a missing
qualification. Build a short, honest, non-defensive answer, and follow it with
what was learned.

## 4. The questions they ask (5-6)
A good question shows the research happened:
- What does success in this role look like in the first six months?
- What is the team wrestling with right now?
- (A specific question about something current that came out of the research)

Do not put salary or leave questions in the first round.

## 5. Output
One file: the company summary · likely questions with short answer notes · the
STAR stories · their questions · logistics (time, link, who is interviewing).
Keep it to something readable in ten minutes before the interview.

## Rules
- Do not make them memorise answers; give a **skeleton**. A memorised answer is
  obvious.
- Do not write an unverified claim about the company.
- After the interview: offer to update the status with `basvuru-takip`.
